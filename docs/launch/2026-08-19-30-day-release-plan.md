# HumanOS Booklet — 30-Day Release Plan
Date: 2026-08-19

## Mission

Publish the first HumanOS booklet in approximately 30 days.

## Constraints

- digital-first
- approximately 50–60 pages
- story-driven
- honest
- no corporate tone
- no unnecessary scope expansion
- finish before attempting perfection

## Weekly Milestones

### Week 1
Editorial structure + major raw drafting.

### Week 2
Complete manuscript.

### Week 3
Structural and voice editing.

### Week 4
Design, QA, launch package, publication.

## Daily Rule

Every booklet working block must produce one visible artifact:

- pages
- chapter
- edit
- visual
- launch asset
- final decision

No research-only sessions unless a specific factual gap blocks writing.

## Definition of Done

A readable, designed, exportable digital first edition exists and can be distributed to readers.
